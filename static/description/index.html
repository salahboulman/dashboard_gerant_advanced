Advanced Manager Dashboard - POS & Stock

A sleek, mobile-friendly web interface for real-time store management

Access your store's live data instantly via a dedicated mobile endpoint (/gerant). Designed specifically for managers and business owners to track sales, stock health, and daily expenses on the go.

Key Features & Endpoint Access

Accessible via Local IP or Localhost

Easily access the manager space directly from any smartphone or tablet connected to your local network using: http://your-server-ip:8069/gerant.

Real-Time KPIs & Filtering

Filter data by specific dates and cashiers instantly. Monitor POS turnover (CA), stock value, total expenses (Masarif), and total orders at a glance.

Stock Alerts & WhatsApp Integration

Immediate visibility on critical inventory shortages (Ruptures) with direct WhatsApp sharing options for smooth team communication.

Quick Expense Tracking (Masarif)

Log petty cash and daily shop expenditures (e.g., rent, supplies) seamlessly right from the mobile interface with instant daily recaps.
